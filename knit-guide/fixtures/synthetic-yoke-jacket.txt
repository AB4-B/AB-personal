Test Yoke Jacket
Designer: Test Designs
Sizes: S - M - L - XL

Finished measurements:
Chest: 96-104-112-120 cm = 37¾"-41"-44"-47¼"

YARN:
Test Wool DK
350-400-450-500 g color 4, brick

BUTTONS:
Test buttons: 4 items in all sizes.

NEEDLES:
Circular needle size 4.5 mm = US 7, length 80 cm = 32".
Circular needle size 3.5 mm = US 4.

KNITTING GAUGE:
19 stitches in width and 26 rows in height with stockinette stitch = 10 x 10 cm = 4" x 4".

EXPLANATIONS:

GARTER STITCH (worked back and forth):
Knit all rows.

BUTTONHOLES:
Work the buttonholes on the right band (when the garment is worn). Work from the right side when there are 4 stitches left on the row as follows: make 1 yarn over, knit 2 together and knit 2. On the next row (wrong side), knit the yarn over so that it leaves a hole.
The first buttonhole is worked 1 cm = ⅜" after the last increase for the V-neck, then the other 3 buttonholes with approx. 6-6-7-7 cm = 2⅜"-2⅜"-2¾"-2¾" between each one.

DECREASE TIP (for sleeves):
Begin 3 stitches before the marker-thread: knit 2 together, knit 2 (the marker-thread sits between these 2 stitches), slip 1 stitch knit-wise, knit 1 and pass the slipped stitch over the knitted stitch (2 stitches decreased).

START THE PIECE HERE:

RIGHT BAND (when the garment is worn):
Cast on 7 stitches with circular needle size 4.5 mm = US 7 and your yarn.
Work GARTER STITCH until the band measures 15-15-16-16 cm = 6"-6"-6¼"-6¼", finishing after a row from the wrong side. Lay the piece to one side.

LEFT BAND:
Cast on 7 stitches with circular needle size 4.5 mm = US 7 and your yarn.
Work garter stitch until the band measures 15-15-16-16 cm = 6"-6"-6¼"-6¼", finishing after a row from the right side. Do not cut the strand.
Cast on 50-50-54-54 stitches at the end of this row, then knit the right band stitches = 64-64-68-68 stitches. Work 1 row with garter stitch over each band and purling the cast-on stitches.

Insert 4 marker-threads (used for the raglan increases), without working the stitches and inserting them between 2 stitches as follows:
Count 9 stitches, insert marker-thread 1, count 16 stitches, insert marker-thread 2, count 14-14-18-18 stitches, insert marker-thread 3, count 16 stitches, insert marker-thread 4, there are 9 stitches left on the row.

YOKE:
Continue back and forth with stockinette stitch and garter stitch over each band. At the same time increase for the V-neck and raglan as described below. Read the next 2 sections before continuing. Start mid-front, from the right side.

V-NECK:
Increase for the neck inside the bands every 4th row 8-8-9-9 times by making 1 yarn over, which is worked twisted on the next row to avoid a hole.
1 cm = ⅜" after the last increase for the neck begin working the BUTTONHOLES - read description above.

RAGLAN:
Increase on each side of 2 stockinette stitches in each transition between the front/back pieces and the sleeves every 2nd row (each row from the right side = 8 increased stitches) 4 times. Continue to increase for raglan every 2nd round but every 2nd increase is only on the front/back pieces (4 increased stitches), i.e., increase on the front/back pieces every 2nd row (each row from the right side) and on the sleeves every 4th row (every 2nd row from the right side). Increase like this 10-12-14-14 times on the body (5-6-7-7 times on the sleeves).

SIZES S and M (the increases in sizes L and XL are finished):
Continue with stockinette stitch and increase for raglan but now only increase on the body every 2nd row (the sleeve increases are finished) 2-1 times.

ALL SIZES:
You have increased a total of 16-17-18-18 times on the body and 9-10-11-11 times on the sleeves.

After the last increase there are 180-188-202-202 stitches. Continue with stockinette stitch and the bands in garter stitch until the yoke measures 20-22-24-24 cm, measured mid-back.

Divide for the body and sleeves as follows:
Work the first 33-34-36-36 stitches (front piece), place the next 34-36-38-38 stitches on a thread for the sleeve, cast on 8-8-10-10 stitches (in side under sleeve), work 46-48-54-54 stitches (back piece), place the next 34-36-38-38 stitches on a thread for the sleeve, cast on 8-8-10-10 stitches (in side under sleeve), work the last 33-34-36-36 stitches (front piece).

BODY:
= 128-132-146-146 stitches. Continue with stockinette stitch and the bands in garter stitch for a further 15-14-13-13 cm. Knit 1 row from the right side and increase 12-12-14-14 stitches evenly spaced (do not increase over the bands) = 140-144-160-160 stitches.
Change to circular needle size 3.5 mm = US 4 and work as follows from the wrong side: 7 band stitches in garter stitch, * purl 1, knit 1 *, work from *-* until there are 8 stitches left, purl 1 and 7 band stitches in garter stitch. Continue this rib for 5 cm. Bind off.

SLEEVES:
Place the 34-36-38-38 stitches from the thread on a short circular needle or double pointed needles size 4.5 mm = US 7 and knit up 1 stitch in each of the 8-8-10-10 stitches cast on under the sleeve = 42-44-48-48 stitches. Insert a marker-thread in the middle of the new stitches under the sleeve.
Work stockinette stitch in the round.
When the sleeve measures 3 cm = 1¼", decrease 2 stitches under the sleeve - read DECREASE TIP. Decrease like this every 4-3-3-3 cm = 1½"-1¼"-1¼"-1¼" a total of 6-7-8-8 times = 30-30-32-32 stitches. Continue working until the sleeve measures 34-33-31-31 cm = 13⅜"-13"-12¼"-12¼" from the division. Knit 1 round and increase 6-6-8-8 stitches evenly spaced = 36-36-40-40 stitches. Change to double pointed needles size 3.5 mm = US 4 and work rib (knit 1, purl 1) for 5 cm. Bind off.
Work the other sleeve in the same way.

ASSEMBLY:
Sew the buttons onto the left band. Sew together the 2 loose bands mid-back and sew them to the neckline.
