Simple Ribbed Scarf
Designer: Test Designs

SIZE: One size, about 20 cm wide and 160 cm long.

YARN:
Test Chunky, 2 skeins.

NEEDLES:
8 mm needles.

GAUGE:
12 stitches and 18 rows in stockinette stitch = 10 x 10 cm.

ABBREVIATIONS:
k - knit
p - purl
RS - right side

SCARF:
Cast on 30 stitches.
Row 1 (RS): k2, * p2, k2 *, repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS): p2, * k2, p2 *, repeat from * to end.
Repeat rows 1-2 until the scarf measures 160 cm.
Bind off in pattern.
Weave in all ends and block lightly.
