Simple Waist Jacket (test pattern)
Designer: Test Designs

SIZE: One size.

YARN:
Test DK, 6 skeins.

NEEDLES:
4 mm circular needle.

GAUGE:
22 stitches and 30 rows in stockinette stitch = 10 x 10 cm.

ABBREVIATIONS:
k - knit
RS - right side

BODY:
Cast on 120 stitches on a circular needle.
Work 6 rows garter st.
When piece measures 6 cm dec 1 st each side and repeat the dec on every 4 cm a total of 3 times = 114 sts.
AT THE SAME TIME when piece measures 20 cm inc 1 st each side and repeat the inc on every 3 cm a total of 2 times = 118 sts.
Bind off when piece measures 40 cm.
