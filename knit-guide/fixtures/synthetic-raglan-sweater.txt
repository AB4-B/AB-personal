Top-down Raglan Pullover
Designer: Test Designs

SIZES: S (M, L, XL)

YARN:
Test Aran, 700 (800, 900, 1000) m.

NEEDLES:
4 mm and 5 mm circular needles, 80 cm.

GAUGE:
18 stitches and 24 rounds = 10 x 10 cm in stockinette stitch on larger needles.

YOKE:
Using the smaller needle cast on 80 (88, 96, 104) sts. Place BOR marker and join in the round.
Work in 1x1 rib (k1, p1) for 2 cm.
Change to the larger needle and knit 1 round.
Marker set-up: [k14 (16, 18, 20), PM, k26 (28, 30, 32), PM] twice.
Round 1: [knit to marker, SM, kfb, knit to 1 st before next marker, kfb, k1, SM] twice, [knit to marker, SM] twice [4 sts inc].
Round 2: knit.
Work rounds 1-2 a total of 10 (12, 14, 16) times. [160 (184, 208, 232) sts]
Work in stockinette stitch until the yoke measures 20 (22, 24, 26) cm from the cast-on edge.

BODY AND SLEEVES:
Separation round: knit to the first marker, place the next 30 sts on hold on waste yarn (sleeve), cast on 6 sts using the backwards loop method, knit to the next marker, place the next 30 sts on hold (sleeve), cast on 6 sts, knit to end.
Change to the smaller needle and work in 1x1 rib for 3 cm.
Bind off all sts loosely.

SLEEVES:
Place the 30 held sts back onto the larger needle. Pick up and knit 6 sts at the underarm, place BOR marker and join to work in the round.
Knit until the sleeve measures 30 cm.
Decrease round: k1, ssk, knit to 3 sts before BOR marker, k2tog, k1 [2 sts dec].
Work 5 rounds even.
Work these 6 rounds a total of 6 times.
Change to the smaller needle and work in 1x1 rib for 5 cm.
Bind off all sts loosely.
Weave in all ends and block.
