Beanie in the Round
Designer: Test Designs

SIZES: Child (Adult S, Adult L)

FINISHED MEASUREMENTS:
Circumference: 46 (52, 58) cm.

YARN:
Test Worsted, 1 skein.

NEEDLES:
4 mm circular needle, 40 cm long, and double pointed needles.

GAUGE:
20 stitches and 28 rounds = 10 x 10 cm in stockinette stitch.

HAT:
Using the smaller needle cast on 88 (100, 112) stitches. Place marker and join to work in the round, being careful not to twist.
Work in 2x2 rib (k2, p2) for 4 cm.
Work in stockinette stitch until the hat measures 14 (16, 18) cm from the cast-on edge.

CROWN:
Decrease round: [k to 2 sts before marker, k2tog] twice.
Work 1 round even.
Work these 2 rounds a total of 8 times.
Cut the yarn, thread it through the remaining stitches and pull tight.
