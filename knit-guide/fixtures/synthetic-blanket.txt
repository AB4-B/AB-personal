Garter Border Baby Blanket
Designer: Test Designs

SIZE: About 80 cm x 90 cm.

YARN:
Test DK, 6 skeins.

NEEDLES:
5 mm circular needle, 80 cm long.

GAUGE:
18 stitches and 36 rows in garter stitch = 10 x 10 cm.

BLANKET:
Cast on 144 stitches using a long-tail cast-on.
Work 8 rows in garter stitch.
Next row (RS): k8, k2tog, yo, knit to the last 8 stitches, yo, ssk, k6.
Row 1 (WS): k8, purl to the last 8 stitches, k8.
Row 2 (RS): knit.
Work rows 1-2 until the blanket measures 85 cm from the cast-on edge.
Work 8 rows in garter stitch.
Bind off all stitches loosely.
Weave in ends. Wet block to the finished size.
